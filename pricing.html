FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why is Free one lesson a day?

Honestly, narration. Every lesson is read aloud, and that voice costs us money the first time a lesson plays. One full lesson a day is what lets us keep the whole library free for everyone.

What counts as a new course?

A topic nobody has asked for yet. When you type something the library doesn’t have, we write a fresh course for it, and on Free that uses one of your three for the month. Reading courses that already exist never counts.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your account page. It takes two taps. Cancel during the 7-day trial and you’re never charged.

What happens when my voice minutes run out?

Text keeps working as normal. If you want to keep talking out loud, add an hour of voice for $5. Your 30 included minutes come back each month.

Is the content accurate?

Courses are written by AI, so they can be wrong. When a lesson shows a number, the source is shown with it. For anything medical, legal or financial, check with a qualified source before you act on it.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, on yearly plans: ask within 14 days of being charged and you get a full refund.
